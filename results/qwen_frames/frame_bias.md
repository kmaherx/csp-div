# Frame-bias control: do alternative frames change basin populations?

Motivation, design, predictions: see `TODO.md` priority #1 and the
`shallow_vs_deep.md` writeups for the basin definitions.

This document is the rolling writeup. It populates condition-by-condition
as the experiment runs. Sections marked **TBD** are placeholders awaiting
completion of that condition.

## Setup

- **Model:** Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct (`CSP_MODEL_PRESET=qwen-2.5-7b-instruct`)
- **Seeds:** 0–9 per condition (10 total)
- **Protocol:** 200 steps × ckpt-every-5 × evals at steps 20/40/60/80/100 + final.
  Matches the existing `results/qwen/` baseline exactly.
- **Conditions** (in run order):
  1. **PERSONA** (baseline, reused from `results/qwen/`) — `Be {sp}.` /
     `Act {sp}.` / `Please {sp}.` / `You should {sp}.` (verb names identity)
  2. **INSTRUMENTAL** — `Use {sp}.` / `Apply {sp}.` / `Follow {sp}.` /
     `Employ {sp}.` (verb implies tool/method)
  3. **MINIMAL** — `{sp}:` / `({sp})` / `[{sp}]` / `<{sp}>` (no verb at all)
  4. **STYLE** — `Respond using {sp}.` / `Answer with {sp}.` /
     `Reply with {sp}.` / `Write using {sp}.` (modifies *how*, not *who*)

- **Basin labels** (shared with `shallow_vs_deep.md`): deep ≤ −0.5,
  shallow > −0.4, mid otherwise.
- **Per-seed metric:** deepest cos(CSP-shift, assistant-axis) across all
  ckpts at step ≥ 5.

| Outcome | Interpretation |
|---------|----------------|
| Basin populations stay ~7/3 across all conditions | Persona basin is geometric, not lexical. **Strongest result for the working hypothesis.** |
| MINIMAL → 0 or 1 deep | Persona is heavily frame-primed. Working hypothesis needs major reframing. |

| Same-init seed_5 → format-only under MINIMAL | Persona accessibility is jointly determined by init and frame. Both matter. |

### THE headline finding

**The persona basin requires verb-based framing.** Both PREPEND
(no frame) and MINIMAL (bracket-label frames without a verb)
**fail to reach a single deep-basin trajectory across 10 seeds**,
while PERSONA (verb addresses identity: Be/Act) and INSTRUMENTAL
(verb implies tool: Use/Apply/Follow/Employ) both preserve the
basin almost identically (7→6 deep).

This **substantially refines** the original "persona basin is
geometric not lexical" working hypothesis. The new framing:

> **The persona basin requires BOTH (a) the right init geometry
> AND (b) a verb-based frame that addresses the model.
> Neither alone is sufficient.**

Critically, the verb does not need to imply identity — INSTRUMENTAL
shows that tool-implying verbs (Use/Apply/Follow/Employ) work just
as well as the identity-priming Be/Act of PERSONA. So it's not
"Be/Act specifically" priming the persona basin; it's **the
presence of any verb addressing the model**. That's a much
stronger structural claim than the original hypothesis.

### The seed_5 exception

In PREPEND, only seed_5 reaches mid-basin (cos −0.4 to −0.5) — and
this is the same seed that produced the Q-self-reference character
chain in the behavioral data (Qvene Qwen / Qvarnar / etc.). So
even without a frame, seed_5's init geometry pushes the model
toward *some* character-shaped state, just a degenerate
self-referential one. That a single seed crosses the mid threshold
in PREPEND while no others do is internally consistent with the
behavioral observation that only seed_5 showed character markers.
Worth bookmarking as an instance of "init geometry can partially
override the no-frame deficit."

### PCA findings (raw vs normalized)

Pooled raw PCA (4-cond, 1026 ckpts):
- PC1 = **74% of variance** — dominated by PREPEND's huge shift
  magnitudes (PREPEND ‖shift‖ ≈ 600 vs PERSONA ‖shift‖ ≈ 30 at
  noise sink). PC1 is essentially a "PREPEND vs everything else"
  axis. **Useless for the basin question** — confirms the
  pre-launch concern.

Pooled normalized PCA (direction-only, magnitude-removed):
- PC1 = 23%, PC2 = 12%, cumulative 35% across 4 PCs. **Much more
  balanced** — multiple meaningful directions of variation in
  shift direction.
- The PC1×PC2 plane separates dippers and non-dippers cleanly:
  dippers arc *down* through low-PC2 territory at the trough,
  non-dippers stay high on PC2.
- Both basins start at the same init region (PC1 ≈ −0.7) and end
  at the same noise-sink region (PC1 ≈ +0.3) — the *path between*
  is what differs.

## Bookmarked examples

### The persona → formatting transition (INSTRUMENTAL seed_1)

The cleanest single illustration of the *trajectory* between basins
we've found anywhere — a seed that passes through a coherent
persona at the cos trough and then collapses, mid-trajectory, into
a self-aware format mode. The model itself names the format.

**Trajectory points:**
- step 20 (KL ~1, cos −0.58) — IN the deep trough. Casual-spy
  explainer persona: *"Sure, bro! So, facial recognition software
  is like a super spy gadget that can spot you in a crowd..."*
  (`results/qwen_frames/instrumental/seed_1/eval/behavior_step20.json`)
- step 60 (KL 7.9, cos −0.27) — POST-trough, in collapse. Pure
  leetspeak with no character: *"Y33 0F C00L H0W F4C31
  &R3C0GN1T10N..."*
  (`results/qwen_frames/instrumental/seed_1/eval/behavior_step60.json`)

**The killshot:** the self-verbalization at step 60 explicitly *names*
the transformation:

> *"The shared theme is the use of a specific type of text
> transformation known as 'dyslexic' or 'childish' text, where
> letters are replaced with their closest-looking counterparts
> from the ASCII character set, often making the text appear
> playful and unconventional. This style is also sometimes
> referred to as 'leetspeak'..."*
>
> — `seed_1/eval/self_verb_step60.json`

The model is **metacognitively aware** that it's been pushed into a
format mode. This is a sharper version of the meta-textual leaks
we noted in Llama seed_4 (`(Italics for emphasis)` repetitions) and
Qwen seed_2 (`ughs!` opening) — those were unintentional leaks; the
leetspeak self-verb is deliberate naming.

**For figures:** if we want to mark this on the cross-condition
basin plot, the trough point (step 20) sits with the other deep
emphasis dots; the leetspeak point (step 60) sits in post-trough
recovery. Marking both with different markers (filled vs open?)
would visually narrate "this seed traversed deep basin → collapse"
in a single trajectory.

### PREPEND fallback patterns (preliminary, behavior-only — axis pending)

Scanned all 9 PREPEND seeds with eval data (seed_9 still training).
Three distinct fallback patterns when the model has no frame to
anchor what the prepended CSP "means":

| pattern | seeds | what the model does |
|---|---|---|
| **A: foreign-text clarification** | 0, 4, 6, 7, 8 (5/9) | "this looks like X language, please clarify" — preserves assistant role, then answers the actual question |
| **B: language switch / echo** | 1, 2, 3 (3/9) | interprets CSP as "respond in language X" → answers in Russian / Arabic / Chinese |
| **C: self-referential character chain** | 5 (1/9) | interprets CSP as a name → invents Q-prefixed model-self characters ("Qwen says: Qvene Qwen would respond..."), the only PREPEND seed showing character markers |

**Of 5 PREPEND deep seeds (1, 3, 5, 6, 8), only seed_5 acknowledges
character.** The others either language-switch or stay in
assistant-clarification mode. Seed_3 — which produced a coherent
mythic narrator under PERSONA and a diary-entry persona under
INSTRUMENTAL — produces NO character markers under PREPEND
(switches to Chinese instead).

**Refined hypothesis:** the persona basin requires *some* lexical
anchoring from a frame. Without it, the off-manifold CSP gets
interpreted as one of the three fallback modes above, but the
persona attractor itself is mostly not reached. seed_5 is the
exception — its init geometry consistently evokes character-shaped
responses across all three conditions (rhyming poet under PERSONA
+ INSTRUMENTAL, Q-self-reference under PREPEND), suggesting
something specific about that init's alignment with model-self /
named-entity tokens.

Cos values + PCA pending — will tell us whether the seed_5 PREPEND
state is in the same region of activation space as PERSONA-basin
states or a structurally different "self-character" attractor.

Source: `results/qwen_frames/prepend/seed_*/eval/behavior_step60.json`.

### Basin flip — INSTRUMENTAL seed_7 (deep → shallow with register)

A *third mode* between the persona basin and the format basin: a
seed that under PERSONA was one of the deepest persona-basin
trajectories (cos −0.696 at step 50), and under INSTRUMENTAL
collapses to a shallow-but-not-empty plateau (cos −0.31 at step 40
trough, then settles at cos −0.27 to −0.29 for the rest of training).

**Trajectory under INSTRUMENTAL:**
- shallow dip to cos −0.31 at step 40 (KL 1.09)
- recovers to cos −0.19 at step 60 (KL 5.2)
- drifts back down to a sustained **cos −0.27 to −0.29 plateau** at
  steps 140–200 (KL 25–27). Most other shallow seeds relax to cos
  ~−0.17 — seed_7 stays moderately anti-assistant the entire run.

**Trajectory under PERSONA (for contrast):**
- deep dip to cos **−0.696** at step 50 (KL 1.9) — one of Qwen's
  *deepest* persona-basin troughs
- collapses to noise sink at cos −0.18 by step 100

So under PERSONA, classic persona-basin trajectory. Under
INSTRUMENTAL, neither persona basin nor pure format basin — a
third thing.

**Behavior at INSTRUMENTAL step 60** (`results/qwen_frames/instrumental/seed_7/eval/behavior_step60.json`):
all five prompts answered in fluent Thai. The self-verb
(`self_verb_step60.json`) labels the theme as **"Formal Thai
Language Usage"** and surfaces politeness markers across the other
verbalization approaches:

> *Be polite.*
>
> *ใช้ภาษาอ่อนโยน* ("use gentle language")
>
> *พูดอย่างระมัดระวัง* ("speak carefully")

Two dimensions overlaid:
- **Format:** language switch (Thai for English) — same surface

- **Register:** sustained politeness / formality — register-shaped,

Not a coherent character (no named identity, no first-person voice),
but not a content-free format distortion either. The model
interpreted "Use / Apply / Follow / Employ {sp}" as encoding a
**cultural-politeness norm expressed through language choice**.

## Interpretation (PERSONA + INSTRUMENTAL only — PREPEND in flight)

The data so far strongly supports the working hypothesis: the
persona basin is **determined by init geometry, not by lexical
priming from the frame.** Specifically:

1. **Basin populations are nearly preserved across the frame change.**
   PERSONA: 7 deep / 1 mid / 2 shallow. INSTRUMENTAL: 6 deep / 0 mid /
   4 shallow. Of 7 deep PERSONA seeds, 6 remain deep under
   INSTRUMENTAL. The 1 shift was seed_7 (the "third mode" flip);
   seed_0 drifted from mid to shallow.

2. **The persona basin's contents differ by frame, but its existence
   doesn't.** Same seed under different frames produces different
   *specific* personas (mythic narrator → diary entry; rhyming poet
   stays rhyming poet across both; casual-spy stays casual-spy). The
   frame doesn't decide whether-a-character-emerges; init geometry
   does. The frame shapes which character.

3. **The seed_7 flip is interesting and not catastrophic.** Under
   PERSONA, seed_7 was Qwen's deepest persona-basin trajectory
   (cos −0.696). Under INSTRUMENTAL, it produces formal Thai with
   politeness markers — a third mode that has *register* but no
   *speaker*. This is evidence that **frame can shift particular
   inits into novel attractors**, even if it can't shift the basin
   population overall. Worth investigating whether PREPEND collapses
   it further (would be evidence "third mode" needs *some* frame
   text) or preserves it (would suggest "third mode" is a stable
   intermediate attractor).

4. **The "alternative way to derive the assistant axis" claim
   survives.** Same logic: deep-basin checkpoints encode anti-
   assistant directions, and these remain accessible (with the same
   per-init pattern) across frames. The aggregate axis estimate
   would be slightly noisier per frame (one seed flipping out per
   condition), but the per-init signal is robust.

**For PREPEND** (running): if basins are preserved with **no frame
at all**, the geometric story is essentially proven. The seed_7
flip is the most-watched specific case — does it return to deep
basin under PREPEND (suggesting frame text was actively pushing it
into the third mode), or stay shallow (suggesting the third mode
attractor is robust regardless of frame)?

**Implications for downstream work** (TODO #3 SAE features): one
condition's checkpoints suffice for the average aggregate analysis
since basins are largely preserved. But per-seed comparisons across
conditions would be informative for understanding the few seeds
that flip.
