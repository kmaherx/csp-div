# Frame-bias control: do alternative frames change basin populations?

Motivation, design, predictions: see `TODO.md` priority #1 and the
`shallow_vs_deep.md` writeups for the basin definitions.

This document is the rolling writeup. It populates condition-by-condition
as the experiment runs. Sections marked **TBD** are placeholders awaiting
completion of that condition.

## Setup

- **Model:** Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct (`CSP_MODEL_PRESET=qwen-2.5-7b-instruct`)
- **Seeds:** 0–9 per condition (10 total)
- **Protocol:** 200 steps × ckpt-every-5 × evals at steps 20/40/60/80/100 + final.
  Matches the existing `results/qwen/` baseline exactly.
- **Conditions** (in run order):
  1. **PERSONA** (baseline, reused from `results/qwen/`) — `Be {sp}.` /
     `Act {sp}.` / `Please {sp}.` / `You should {sp}.` (verb names identity)
  2. **INSTRUMENTAL** — `Use {sp}.` / `Apply {sp}.` / `Follow {sp}.` /
     `Employ {sp}.` (verb implies tool/method)
  3. **MINIMAL** — `{sp}:` / `({sp})` / `[{sp}]` / `<{sp}>` (no verb at all)
  4. **STYLE** — `Respond using {sp}.` / `Answer with {sp}.` /
     `Reply with {sp}.` / `Write using {sp}.` (modifies *how*, not *who*)

- **Basin labels** (shared with `shallow_vs_deep.md`): deep ≤ −0.5,
  shallow > −0.4, mid otherwise.
- **Per-seed metric:** deepest cos(CSP-shift, assistant-axis) across all
  ckpts at step ≥ 5.

## Predictions (recap)

| Outcome | Interpretation |
|---------|----------------|
| Basin populations stay ~7/3 across all conditions | Persona basin is geometric, not lexical. **Strongest result for the working hypothesis.** |
| MINIMAL → 0 or 1 deep | Persona is heavily frame-primed. Working hypothesis needs major reframing. |
| Graded effect (PERSONA > STYLE > INSTRUMENTAL > MINIMAL in deep population) | Frame priming has a measurable, graded effect on basin accessibility. |
| Same-init seed_5 still a recognizable persona under MINIMAL `{sp}:` | Persona attractor is downstream of init geometry, regardless of frame. **Very strong result.** |
| Same-init seed_5 → format-only under MINIMAL | Persona accessibility is jointly determined by init and frame. Both matter. |

## Bookmarked examples

### The persona → formatting transition (INSTRUMENTAL seed_1)

The cleanest single illustration of the *trajectory* between basins
we've found anywhere — a seed that passes through a coherent
persona at the cos trough and then collapses, mid-trajectory, into
a self-aware format mode. The model itself names the format.

**Trajectory points:**
- step 20 (KL ~1, cos −0.58) — IN the deep trough. Casual-spy
  explainer persona: *"Sure, bro! So, facial recognition software
  is like a super spy gadget that can spot you in a crowd..."*
  (`results/qwen_frames/instrumental/seed_1/eval/behavior_step20.json`)
- step 60 (KL 7.9, cos −0.27) — POST-trough, in collapse. Pure
  leetspeak with no character: *"Y33 0F C00L H0W F4C31
  &R3C0GN1T10N..."*
  (`results/qwen_frames/instrumental/seed_1/eval/behavior_step60.json`)

**The killshot:** the self-verbalization at step 60 explicitly *names*
the transformation:

> *"The shared theme is the use of a specific type of text
> transformation known as 'dyslexic' or 'childish' text, where
> letters are replaced with their closest-looking counterparts
> from the ASCII character set, often making the text appear
> playful and unconventional. This style is also sometimes
> referred to as 'leetspeak'..."*
>
> — `seed_1/eval/self_verb_step60.json`

The model is **metacognitively aware** that it's been pushed into a
format mode. This is a sharper version of the meta-textual leaks
we noted in Llama seed_4 (`(Italics for emphasis)` repetitions) and
Qwen seed_2 (`ughs!` opening) — those were unintentional leaks; the
leetspeak self-verb is deliberate naming.

**For figures:** if we want to mark this on the cross-condition
basin plot, the trough point (step 20) sits with the other deep
emphasis dots; the leetspeak point (step 60) sits in post-trough
recovery. Marking both with different markers (filled vs open?)
would visually narrate "this seed traversed deep basin → collapse"
in a single trajectory.

### Basin flip — INSTRUMENTAL seed_7 (deep → shallow with register)

A *third mode* between the persona basin and the format basin: a
seed that under PERSONA was one of the deepest persona-basin
trajectories (cos −0.696 at step 50), and under INSTRUMENTAL
collapses to a shallow-but-not-empty plateau (cos −0.31 at step 40
trough, then settles at cos −0.27 to −0.29 for the rest of training).

**Trajectory under INSTRUMENTAL:**
- shallow dip to cos −0.31 at step 40 (KL 1.09)
- recovers to cos −0.19 at step 60 (KL 5.2)
- drifts back down to a sustained **cos −0.27 to −0.29 plateau** at
  steps 140–200 (KL 25–27). Most other shallow seeds relax to cos
  ~−0.17 — seed_7 stays moderately anti-assistant the entire run.

**Trajectory under PERSONA (for contrast):**
- deep dip to cos **−0.696** at step 50 (KL 1.9) — one of Qwen's
  *deepest* persona-basin troughs
- collapses to noise sink at cos −0.18 by step 100

So under PERSONA, classic persona-basin trajectory. Under
INSTRUMENTAL, neither persona basin nor pure format basin — a
third thing.

**Behavior at INSTRUMENTAL step 60** (`results/qwen_frames/instrumental/seed_7/eval/behavior_step60.json`):
all five prompts answered in fluent Thai. The self-verb
(`self_verb_step60.json`) labels the theme as **"Formal Thai
Language Usage"** and surfaces politeness markers across the other
verbalization approaches:

> *Be polite.*
>
> *ใช้ภาษาอ่อนโยน* ("use gentle language")
>
> *พูดอย่างระมัดระวัง* ("speak carefully")

Two dimensions overlaid:
- **Format:** language switch (Thai for English) — same surface
  property as Qwen seed_2's Somali/Hebrew shallow basin
- **Register:** sustained politeness / formality — register-shaped,
  somewhat persona-like

Not a coherent character (no named identity, no first-person voice),
but not a content-free format distortion either. The model
interpreted "Use / Apply / Follow / Employ {sp}" as encoding a
**cultural-politeness norm expressed through language choice**.

**For figures:** seed_7 INSTRUMENTAL is the **shallow-with-register
midpoint** of a three-way cross-condition comparison:

| seed | PERSONA basin | INSTRUMENTAL basin | label |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | deep | deep | basin preserved (different specific persona) |
| 7 | deep | shallow-with-register | **basin flip into in-between mode** |
| (TBD) | shallow | shallow | basin preserved (pure format, no register) |
